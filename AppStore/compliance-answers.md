## Content rights

Question: Does the app contain, show, or access third-party content?

Recommended conservative answer: **Yes**.

Explanation if requested: optional Voice Mode accesses transcripts and structured responses generated by OpenAI at the user's request. The app does not redistribute a third-party media catalog. The developer and user must comply with OpenAI's applicable terms, and the app uses only its own artwork, interface, and marketing assets.

If App Store Connect frames the question specifically around licensed media catalogs rather than any service output, review the exact wording before changing this answer.

## Export compliance

- The app uses HTTPS, TLS, and Apple Keychain through Apple-provided operating-system functionality.
- The app does not implement proprietary or non-standard cryptography.
- `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is set to `false` in `Info.plist`.
- Expected result: no export-compliance document upload is required.

Answer any App Store Connect follow-up according to the actual binary. Do not claim that the app contains no encryption at all: it uses exempt/system encryption for network and credential security.

## Accessibility Nutrition Labels

Accessibility labels are currently voluntary, but App Store Connect will show “Support not yet indicated” until answers are published.

Safe answer based on the audited build:

- Device: iPhone
- Does the app support any listed accessibility features?: Yes
- Dark Interface: Yes

Do **not** claim the following until all common tasks have been tested against Apple's criteria on a physical iPhone:

- VoiceOver
- Voice Control
- Larger Text
- Differentiate Without Color Alone
- Sufficient Contrast
- Reduced Motion
- Captions
- Audio Descriptions

The app uses SwiftUI accessibility semantics in several key controls and honors Reduce Motion in several animated views, but that is not sufficient evidence that every common task meets Apple's product-page claim criteria. Captions and audio descriptions do not apply because Mäuse contains no prerecorded video or audio content.

## EU Digital Services Act

Account-holder decision supplied July 12, 2026: **EU non-trader**.

Select **This is a non-trader provider** (or the equivalent current wording) for Mäuse in App Store Connect. Apple will inform EU customers that consumer rights arising from contracts with traders do not apply. No public trader address or public trader phone should be entered for this app.

This is separate from the private App Review contact phone, which is entered only in review information and is not displayed on the product page.
