# Mäuse 1.5.0 (49) — internal TestFlight

## Scope

Apple on-device voice capture is selectable alongside the existing OpenAI option. The local path uses Apple Speech with both an on-device support check and `requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`, followed by `SystemLanguageModel.default`. It never creates an OpenAI service or falls back to one. The iOS 17 deployment target is retained; Apple mode is gated to iOS 26+ and runtime model/language readiness.

The setup and consent text explain free local processing, hardware and download requirements, shorter turn-based capture, cloud billing, and privacy in English and German. New installs have Apple selected with voice off; existing settings retain OpenAI. Provider changes revoke consent and disable capture.

The model selects the relevant words and request intent. The app controls draft identity and converts source-grounded prices, dates, and shares. All drafts remain subject to review and explicit Save.
