# Mäuse brand assets

## Assets

- `MaeuseBrandMark.png`: transparent standalone mouse/cheese mark for flexible placement.
- `MaeuseLogoLockupLight.png`: transparent horizontal lockup for light backgrounds.
- `MaeuseLogoLockupDark.png`: transparent horizontal lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `MaeuseBrandBackdropLight.png`: reusable light brand backdrop at 1600 × 900.
- `MaeuseBrandBackdropDark.png`: reusable dark brand backdrop at 1600 × 900.
- `../Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png`: App Store icon master.
- `MaeuseAppIconDark.png`: dark-appearance App Store icon master.

## Core colors

- Cheese: `#FFCF3F`
- Cream: `#FFF6DE`
- Ink: `#241C05`
- Light cheese: `#FFF1C2`
- Dark background: `#211A09`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to roughly half the height of its euro symbol. Prefer the ink lockup on light backgrounds and the cream lockup on dark backgrounds.
